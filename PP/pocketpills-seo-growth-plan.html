GEO / AEO strategy — Generative & Answer Engine Optimisation

You're already in ChatGPT. Now make it deliberate.

You're seeing conversions from ChatGPT with zero tracking infrastructure. This means you're being cited in AI answers by accident — likely because you have real drug pricing data. The opportunity is to systematically optimise for every major AI engine: Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, ChatGPT, Claude, and Bing Copilot.

Google AI Overviews — optimisation checklist
FAQPage schema on every drug and condition page
FAQ schema is the primary signal Google uses to extract content into AI Overviews. Every drug question — "how does ozempic work", "is semaglutide safe", "how to get a prescription online" — triggers an AI Overview. Without schema markup, your content cannot be machine-extracted. Target: 100% of drug and condition pages have FAQPage schema by end of month 2.
Direct answers in the first 100–150 words of every article
AI Overviews pull from the opening paragraph, not the conclusion. Every article should open with a 2–3 sentence direct answer to the title question — then elaborate. "Ozempic costs between $250–$400/month in Canada without insurance. Most provincial drug plans do not cover it for weight loss alone..." — then provide the full article. This structure also improves featured snippet eligibility.
HowTo schema for all process and instructional content
"How to transfer a prescription", "How to get Ozempic in Canada", "How to inject Ozempic", "How to store Wegovy" — all need HowTo schema with numbered steps. Google renders these as visual step-by-step sequences in AI Overviews, featured snippets, and voice search results. These formats drive significantly higher CTR than standard blue links.
ChatGPT, Perplexity, and other AI engines
Build comprehensive reference pages for each drug
AI engines heavily weight comprehensive, well-structured reference pages — the kind of content you'd expect on a government or academic health site. Create definitive reference pages for each major drug: detailed, neutral, well-cited, updated with Health Canada approval dates and regulatory changes. These become the canonical sources that AI engines pull from repeatedly.
Get cited by the health media AI engines trust most
ChatGPT and Perplexity preferentially cite CBC Health, Globe & Mail, Healthline, and major .ca health publications over pharmacy sites directly. Your PR strategy (pitching data stories to these outlets) serves double duty: backlinks for SEO + citation sources for GEO. When CBC writes "according to Pocketpills data...", AI engines see it and cite it.
Structured pricing data for drug cost queries
You have unique, real-time pricing data no competitor has at scale. Add Product/Offer schema to drug pages with current Canadian pricing. Perplexity in particular surfaces price data in answers to cost queries. "How much does Wegovy cost in Canada" with structured Product schema = direct citation opportunity. Your /search-prices page is already close — add the schema layer.
